My favorite and delicious lasagna

My Favorite and Delicious Lasagna is a special family favorite that brings everyone together around the dinner table. It’s the perfect meal for Friday nights, family dinners, or any day I feel like making something delicious for the people I love. For me, lasagna is more than just food; it’s about happiness, family time, and sharing a meal that everyone enjoys.

List of Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 jar (24 oz) pasta sauce
  • 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped (optional)

Cook the noodles

  1. Boil the water: Bring water to a boil in a medium-sized pot.
  2. Cook the noodles: Add the lasagna noodles and cook for about 15 minutes, or until tender.
  3. Rinse with cold water: Drain the noodles and rinse them with cold water to stop the cooking process.
  4. Lay out the noodles: Place the noodles flat on a sheet of parchment paper to prevent them from sticking together.

Making the Ground Beef Sauce

  1. Heat the oil: Add 2 tablespoons of olive oil to a large pan and heat over medium heat.
  2. Cook the ground beef: Add 1 pound of ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it into small pieces with a spoon.
  3. Add the onion and garlic: Add 1 chopped onion and 2–3 minced garlic cloves. Cook for 2–3 minutes until softened and fragrant.
  4. Season the beef: Add 1 teaspoon of salt, ½ teaspoon of black pepper, 1 teaspoon of Italian seasoning, and ½ teaspoon of paprika. Stir everything together.
  5. Add the tomato sauce: Pour in 1 can (15 ounces) of tomato sauce and 1 can (28 ounces) of crushed tomatoes. Stir well to combine.
  6. Add extra flavor: Add 1 tablespoon of tomato paste and 1 teaspoon of sugar (optional) to balance the acidity of the tomatoes.
  7. Simmer the sauce: Reduce the heat to low and let the sauce simmer for 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens and the flavors blend together.
  8. Taste and adjust: Taste the sauce and add more salt, pepper, or Italian seasoning if needed. Remove it from the heat and let it cool slightly before assembling the lasagna.

Assembling and Baking the Lasagna

  1. Preheat the oven: Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Prepare the cheese mixture: In a bowl, mix 1 container (15 ounces) of ricotta cheese with 1 egg and ¼ cup of grated Parmesan cheese. Stir until well combined.
  3. Prepare the baking dish: Spread a thin layer of ground beef sauce on the bottom of a baking dish to prevent the noodles from sticking.
  4. Add the first layer of noodles: Arrange the cooked lasagna noodles over the sauce in a single layer.
  5. Add the cheese mixture: Spread a layer of the ricotta cheese mixture evenly over the noodles.
  6. Add the meat sauce: Spoon the ground beef sauce over the ricotta cheese layer.
  7. Add mozzarella cheese: Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the meat sauce.
  8. Repeat the layers: Continue layering the noodles, ricotta mixture, ground beef sauce, and mozzarella cheese until all the ingredients are used.
  9. Finish the top layer: Place a final layer of noodles on top, cover with the remaining ground beef sauce, and sprinkle generously with shredded mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Cover and bake: Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  11. Uncover and finish baking: Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes, or until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly golden.
  12. Let it rest: Remove the lasagna from the oven and let it rest for 10–15 minutes before cutting and serving. This helps the layers hold together.
